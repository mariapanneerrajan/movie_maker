# 04 — Title

## Set 1

1. **THE BROKEN SHAFT** — *object.* A machine fails and a man's work is finished → the piece
   that snapped is the piece that opened the ground; the break was the tool all along.
2. **THE BLACK DUNE** — *image.* The one place on the map nobody will go near → the ground
   nobody wanted was the ground with the whole river under it.
3. **ROOM ENOUGH** — *whisper.* A man asking for a small patch of his own → what opened for
   him was wider than everything that was taken, and it reached the takers too.
4. **THE RIVER THEY BURIED** — *turn.* Somebody shovelled a man's water out of sight →
   nothing they buried was wasted; it came up somewhere else, bigger, and ran back to green
   their own dry flags.
5. **FLAGS CANNOT DRINK** — *wild.* A strange, aphoristic dare → you can own the sand and
   still be thirsty; the water goes where it goes, and it goes to everyone.

Recommendation: **THE RIVER THEY BURIED** — the only candidate built from the turn rather than
the setting; puts a hook question in the thumbnail and hands the closing overhead its second
meaning for free. Runners-up: *The Black Dune* most legible at thumbnail size but names a place,
not a change; *Flags Cannot Drink* most distinctive in a feed but slightly points at the rivals,
whom the film never villainizes.

*(Set 1 not selected — Rajan steered toward the direct-encouragement register instead. Note:
written before the flag-free, grass-free ending; #4's "greened their flags" payoff and #5's
closing flags no longer exist on screen.)*

## Set 2 — the "Don't Give Up" register (direct encouragement, spoken to the viewer)

Seed: Rajan — "what about the title 'Don't Give Up'? Can you give me some suggestions similar
to this?"

1. **DON'T GIVE UP** — *line, Rajan's own.* The plainest possible promise → unchanged after the
   film. Warmest and most legible of the six, but the only one with no second meaning, no image
   the film owns, and shared with thousands of songs and clips — it disappears in a feed.
2. **KEEP DIGGING** — *line.* Keep going when it costs you → the third shaft, and the two
   buried holes that were never wasted work.
3. **ONE MORE TURN** — *wild.* One more attempt after you were ready to stop → the crank arm he
   walks round, and the last image: soaked, arms wide, turning slowly under the rain.
4. **THEY CAN'T BURY WATER** — *turn.* What is truly yours cannot be taken → buried twice, and
   the third time it comes up taller than the sand did.
5. **YOUR GROUND IS WAITING** — *line.* A place meant for you, still unclaimed → the black dune
   nobody came back for, where no flag ever arrives.
6. **IT'S STILL DOWN THERE** — *line.* The thing you stopped hoping for hasn't gone anywhere →
   the sand-vein under dead black sand, under every hole they filled.

Recommendation: **KEEP DIGGING** — the encouragement wanted, two words, unbreakable at
thumbnail size, and unlike *Don't Give Up* it is the film's literal action, gaining its second
meaning when the column climbs. Caveat: "when you're in a hole, stop digging" gives it a faint
ironic edge for some viewers — **DIG AGAIN** is the same title with the irony removed and the
three-times pattern built in.

## CHOSEN — 2026-08-15

**DON'T GIVE UP** (Set 2, #1 — Rajan's own candidate, selected over the recommendation.)
