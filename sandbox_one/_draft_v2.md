## BEAT SHEET

**LOGLINE** *(as approved at gate 1, verbatim)*
When a garbage giant rises from the Cooum and the city's armoured response force empties every
weapon into it without effect, a slender conservancy worker refuses the exo-suit they offer him,
wades into the black water for five sorted things, and aims for the one seam that armour was never
covering.

**SCRIPTURE ANCHOR — header only. Not quoted, not paraphrased, not spoken.**
- **1 Samuel 17:38–40**, with **17:34–37** and **17:45** — he refuses the armour; he has already done
  this small; he does not come with sword and spear.
- **Romans 5:20** — *where sin abounded.* The film's strongest theological image is that force
  applied to the consequence of sin **increases it**: every round fired becomes new plating.
  Downstream stages must not flatten node 3 into a stock "weapons don't work" beat. The point is not
  that the weapons fail. The point is that they **feed it**.
- **Psalm 118:22 / Matthew 21:42** — the fifth thing is the one piece of refuse that fits none of the
  five bins. **This is theme only. It is never the mechanism.**
- **ORDER OF OPERATIONS, binding on every downstream stage: rescue first, labour second.** The city
  never earns the fall of a giant. Nobody in this film brings a giant down — not the response force,
  not the crowd, not Arul. Arul opens his hand and the giant takes the thing itself.

**THEME (for the room, not the film)**
Faith is not the absence of fear. It is the small clean careful thing done while afraid, by the
person nobody sent for, with something he did not go and get.

**THE SECOND TRUE THING**
The giant is made of what the city threw away and what it quietly piped out of sight. It was made by
a thousand small carelessnesses — and it is **not** unmade by a thousand small labours. It is unmade
by one open hand. Then the city is given the work of carrying away what it can no longer pretend it
did not make, and that work is gratitude, not payment.

**THE RULE — one sentence, stated once, rehearsed in node 1 and obeyed in node 5**

> **Rot is the glue: mixed waste binds because the wet welds it, and anything washed and dried by
> hand carries none of that, so the mass cannot bind through it and comes apart along its own
> materials.**

The giants are single unsorted knots, so force only feeds them. The breathing seam at the base of
the throat is the one place plating can never cover, because it must open to feed.

**FEELING AS THE CREDITS LAND**
Awe at the scale, relief at the fall, a laugh in the montage, and then the pleasant sting of being
implicated.

**STRUCTURAL DEVIATION (unchanged, upheld in round 1).** craft-story's node 3 is the protagonist's
attempt. Here it is **the city's** attempt, and Arul's job in it is to be waved aside. His exclusion
is the engine; his one attempt begins at 2:00.

## HEAD WRITER'S NOTES ON v2

**The second giant — how it landed, for scripture-guardian's re-read.** At 2:30 the clean light out
of the seam **does not stop at the giant's edge** — it runs on, north, over the ground, faster than
people. At 2:47 the Kodungaiyur giant is **still standing** and the city turns toward it, so
entertainment-doctor's stake is live and the audience does the arithmetic. At 2:48, **before the
nearest of them is within a kilometre**, the light reaches it and it resolves on the horizon. The
crowd is still walking; some of them stop walking to watch. Then they arrive, and what they do is
**carry** — gathering what has already been sorted. **No human being in this film brings down a
giant.** Arul does not either; he opens his hand and the giant takes the thing itself. The one act of
force in the film is the barrage, and it makes the giant bigger.

**DECLINED:** entertainment-doctor #5's specific request that *the city* bring the second one down.
It would make the film's last statement "we fixed it ourselves," the exact inversion of Ephesians
2:8–9 that holds the veto — and it would make the ending smaller, because a second fight is a repeat,
and repeats are where three-minute films die.

**DECLINED:** fact-checker #7 Option B (unsortability as the mechanism). Unsortability causing
sorting is not physics, and making the unbinnable wrapper the *mechanism* would tell a family
audience that the un-recyclable thing is the useful one, four seconds before a city sorts its waste.
Psalm 118:22 stays where it belongs: theme, not engineering.

**DECLINED:** fact-checker #9 fix (b) (the pouch *is* the fifth stone). It restores exactly the
self-sourcing scripture-guardian #2 holds a veto against.
