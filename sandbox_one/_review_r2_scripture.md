# Round 2 — scripture-guardian (VETO HOLDER)

**Draft reviewed:** `_draft_v2.md`
**VERDICT: PASS — nothing is blocking. The veto is released.**

**SCRIPTURE ANCHOR AS WRITTEN:** A man is stripped of everything he brought — the armour he refuses,
the pouch the river empties, and the four things his own skill retrieves, all of which fail — until
the only thing left is one he did not go and get and does not throw but releases, and the rescue that
follows outruns him to a giant he never touched, so that the city's labour afterward stands in
exactly the position Ephesians 2:10 stands to Ephesians 2:8–9: **the work walked in, never the price
paid.**

## Why each round-1 finding cleared

**#1 [BLOCKING] works-righteousness — CLEARS, genuinely, not by fig leaf.** I read the last thirty
seconds cold. The test for a fig leaf is whether the film's *emotional weight* still credits the
crowd while a technicality credits something else. It does not. The emotional peak of node 6 is
2:30–2:41 — the resolving, the boy set down, Meera's two gauntlets — all of it before a single sack
is filled. The crowd sequence is four seconds and its internal beat is a **reversal**: they set out
to do a thing and are relieved of it before they arrive. Crucially, the film spent nodes 3 and 4
establishing that human force *feeds* the giant and that armour cannot pick up a bottle, so a crowd
walking north with sacks was never framed as competence in the first place. "Some of them stop
walking to watch" is doing exactly the work the head writer claims for it — that is Exodus 14:13
followed by 14:15, witness first and then go forward — and it is right that only *some* stop. Making
the whole crowd stop would have been the pious version and worse. The added line in THE SECOND TRUE
THING ("that work is gratitude, not payment") and the binding ORDER OF OPERATIONS header close the
gap between the notes and the film that was the actual defect in v1.

**#2 [STRONG] the deletable prayer — CLEARS, and lands harder than what I asked for.** The ratio is
not merely four-plus-one; the four found by skill are each shown failing, in ascending proximity,
until he is standing directly underneath with nothing. He is emptied of the armour, then the pouch,
then his own retrieval — **three separate strippings** — and what works is the one thing that arrived
against the back of his hand, released rather than thrown. The prayer is no longer deletable: cut
"Help me find them" and the unsought fifth loses its addressee. The film's answer to "what saves this
city" is now a gift, and it survives scrutiny. 1 Samuel 17:47 is intact.

**#3 [STRONG] "completely certain" — CLEARS.** Gone; "he is not sure of himself, and he says it
anyway" is the correct distinction between certainty of diagnosis and certainty of cure. Node 1's
"Nothing happens. He waits. He is not certain." and 2:29's "He shuts his eyes, because he does not
know" bracket the film with unknowing at both ends. Hebrews 11:8 is honoured. **I withdraw** the note
about his face at "Then you wear it" — v2 stages Meera's face there instead and leaves his neutral,
which is sufficient.

**#4 Romans 5:20 — ACCEPTED into the header** with a gloss that tells downstream stages the point is
not that the weapons fail but that they *feed it*.

## What the revision introduced

**The light running north is the strongest new thing in the draft and it is theologically sound:** it
originates in the seam that *received* a gift, no one aims it, no one summons it, no one can outrun
it, and it reaches a giant Arul never touched. That is grace exceeding the reach of the one who
released it, represented worthily — unnamed, unmanipulated, not a plot device anyone bargains with.
It is earned by the film's own established mechanism, so it is not a deus ex machina; it is
double-readable as physics or as gift, **which is what "woven" means.**

**The outfall-capping and aeration at 2:51 SUPPORT rather than dilute.** The film established the
outfall running at 2:08, during Arul's most vulnerable moment, as an ongoing cause. Capping it is not
the city buying anything back — **it is the city ceasing to do the thing, which is repentance in its
plain sense**, and it sits correctly in the response half. It also repairs a real weakness in v1,
where picking up litter appeared to clean a sewage-fouled river.

## Under-weaving vs preaching

The revision has drifted in **neither** direction. Dialogue went *down*, seven lines from eight; no
character states the theme; nothing is quoted on screen; the only God-ward gesture is a four-word
inaudible line and one upward look "at nothing anyone else can see." **The weave got denser without a
single overt addition**, which is the harder and better outcome and exactly what I hoped for. All
header citations — 1 Samuel 17:34–37 / 38–40 / 45, Romans 5:20, Psalm 118:22 with Matthew 21:42 —
are accurate and in context, and the head writer's decision to decline fact-checker #7 Option B was
itself a protection of Psalm 118:22 from being bent into a mechanism it does not mean. **I back that
refusal.**

## Two things I would defend against any later reviewer

- **The twenty exo-frames kneeling to hold sacks open at 2:44** — armour useless for the small work
  made useful for service. Isaiah 2:4.
- **Kavi's arc**, where the boy who dropped the first wrapper is coughed loose unharmed *before* he
  does anything, and only then becomes the one who catches the last one. That ordering is correct and
  should not be shuffled at gate 4.
