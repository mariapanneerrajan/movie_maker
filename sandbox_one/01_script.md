                                 ACT ONE


FADE IN:


1.                                             0:00-0:06

EXT. JUNGLE CLEARING - MIDDAY

Clear shallow water running over black stones. Sunlight
crawls on the sand between them.

The whole stream JOLTS. Stones and water leap up together
and RATTLE down.

A shadow slides across the surface. It jolts again, harder.

We CRANE UP -- out of the water, past a leg of fitted
basalt standing in the current, steam boiling white off it,
up twelve metres of stone body seamed with molten orange.
THE STONE BEAST turns its head. Two furnace slits.

The crane keeps going and the clearing opens: flowering
trees, hanging vine, orchids in a dozen colours, a green
wall climbing out of the top of the frame.

RAJAN runs through the shallows into the light. Lean, hide
wrap, ochre scarf, a stone hammer cocked back over his
shoulder. He is the size of the giant's hand.

He swings.


                                 ACT TWO


2.                                             0:06-0:12

EXT. JUNGLE CLEARING - MIDDAY

SLOW MOTION. The hammer head meets the beast's forearm and
BURSTS -- stone chips and spray blowing back through the
sunlight. Rajan is left holding a bare haft.

Real time. We ORBIT them, low over the water. Cinders shed
off the giant's shoulders and HISS out in the stream, black
before they settle.

He throws the haft away, tears a flint spear off his back,
plants it in the gravel and drives the point into the
glowing seam behind the giant's knee.

The flint crumbles. The shaft SNAPS.

The orbit brings the beast's arm around behind him, wide
and slow and enormous.


3.                                             0:12-0:18

EXT. JUNGLE CLEARING - MIDDAY

SLOW MOTION. Rajan comes off his feet and travels, small
under the canopy, through a curtain of leaves and torn
flowers.

He hits the shallows. Skids. Water and black stones fan out
ahead of him.

The camera sinks to the surface beside his face. He lifts
his head, and past his cheek, across the clearing, lies a
fallen forest giant -- a trunk wider than the beast is
tall, gone soft with moss.

Standing up out of the whole length of it, in a row, young
trees in full flower.

His breathing changes. His hand crawls forward under the
water and closes on one black stone.


4.                                             0:18-0:24

EXT. JUNGLE CLEARING - MIDDAY

He pushes up to one knee in the current. Drags the ochre
scarf off his neck. The stone drops into the fold.

The water jumps. The beast wades in, and the sunlight goes
out of the clearing behind it.

He starts the scarf turning. Once. Twice. A rising WHIRR.

The fist comes down beside him and CRATERS the streambed --
water, gravel and black stone blown straight up. He goes
over backward into the shallows.

The scarf is still in his fist.


                                ACT THREE


5.                                             0:24-0:28

EXT. JUNGLE CLEARING - MIDDAY

He rolls out of the water and surges up onto one knee, and
the scarf comes around with him -- the rise and the whirl
are one movement. At the top of it he lets go.

SLOW MOTION. One black stone climbs out of the shade into
the light, and we ride it up.

CRACK.

Darkness runs out of the impact and down the giant's seams,
one after another, until nothing in it is burning. Twelve
metres of stone goes over backward and lands in the stream
-- BOOM -- and a wave walks out across the clearing.

Then nothing but birds.


6.                                             0:28-0:30

EXT. JUNGLE CLEARING - MIDDAY

Water level. The same black stones, settling. The wave
slides away and the surface clears. Beyond it lies a huge
grey hand, half in the current, unlit.

The camera RISES as Rajan stands, scarf hanging from his
fist. Sunlight pours back into the clearing. Beside his
foot a flattened fern lifts itself upright, and above him
the green goes up and up.
