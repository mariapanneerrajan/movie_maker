# LOOK — THE SMALLEST STONE

**Logline:** In a lush, blazing-green daylight jungle, a magma-veined stone beast breaks caveman
Rajan's hammer and throws him down into a shallow stream of cooled black stones — until one stone,
slung from his scarf, brings the giant down. No crowd, no other characters.

**Anchor:** The shepherd's stone and the giant's fall — the smallest thing on the field ends the
largest, and everything that had been pinned in place gets up afterward. Carried in symbol only:
the **fallen forest giant**, a trunk wider than the beast is tall, long down and gone soft with
moss, with a row of young flowering trees standing up out of its whole length; the **stone is the
beast's own cooled cinder**, shed hissing into the stream in shot 2; and the **flattened fern that
lifts itself upright** in the closing image. The label never appears, on screen or in any
downstream prompt.

**Feeling to leave behind:** The surge of being flat on your back under something that cannot be
beaten, and standing up over it anyway — with the smallest thing in your hand.

## Colour script
- **Act One (0:00–0:06):** the jungle at full, luscious saturation — emerald and jade canopy, lime
  backlit leaves, magenta and orange orchids, turquoise water over black stone, molten orange
  seams, one ochre-red scarf. Hot, high-key, everything clearly legible.
- **Act Two (0:06–0:24):** the colour is never drained out of the world — it is progressively
  *covered*. The beast's shadow, blown spray, steam and leaf-debris close over the frame edges
  while the greens deepen toward cool jade-grey and the water loses its sparkle. The beast's orange
  becomes the brightest thing on screen. Bottom is at the crater, end of shot 4: cool jade shade,
  the scarf down to dull brick — but still open daylight, still readable, colour still alive at the
  frame edges.
- **Act Three (0:24–0:30):** the orange dies out of the giant seam by seam and the same warmth
  returns as sunlight through the canopy. The closing image restores Act One's palette exactly —
  with the heat now on Rajan instead of on the giant.
- The single node's continuity block states all three states and the switch points: Act One palette
  holds through **shot 1**; the covering begins at **shot 2** and bottoms at the CRATER at the end
  of **shot 4**; warmth returns at the CRACK inside **shot 5**; full Act One palette restored at
  **shot 6**.

## Grade and render character
Large-format digital, filmic daylight response — deep highlight roll-off, natural contrast, no
crushed blacks anywhere (Rajan's daylight rule is absolute). Fine grain. Texture over grime: wet
leaf sheen, basalt slab grain, pollen and spray suspended in the light shafts, moss, orchid petals
on the water.

## Lighting design
One sun, high, dropping shafts through gaps in the canopy; the stream throws caustic light back up
onto leaves, on Rajan's face and on the giant's underside, so nothing ever goes to silhouette. The
giant's molten seams are the film's second light and they are a **competing, false light** — they
grow brighter as its body blocks the sun out of the clearing, and they go out at the climax. The
sun that was there the whole time is what reaches Rajan at the end.

## Camera rule
**Height belongs to whoever is winning, and the camera makes exactly one move per shot.** It cranes
up for the giant (1), orbits at eye level while the fight is even (2), sinks to water level as
Rajan goes down (3–4), rides the stone up at the climax (5), and makes its final rise with Rajan
standing (6).

## Lens language
- **Act One, shot 1 — 84° wide:** the giant and the canopy stack the full height of the vertical
  frame; Rajan reads clearly but hand-sized at the bottom.
- **Act Two, shots 2–3 (action) — 47° standard:** grounded and natural; impact, weight, spray and
  distance read true through the orbit and the throw.
- **Act Two, shot 3 (water) and shot 4 — 29° short telephoto:** jungle compresses into soft green
  bokeh; his face, the stone and the turning scarf pop sharp against it.
- **Act Three, shot 5 — 18° telephoto:** the stone and the giant's brow compress onto one plane,
  and the fall reads as a wall coming down toward camera.
- **Act Three, shot 6 — 84° wide:** one figure, full canopy height above him.

## Cast locks
- **RAJAN (`CHR_RAJAN`)** — in-story name **Rajan**. Late 30s, 178 cm, lean and wiry, narrow
  shoulders, no bulk. Medium-deep South Asian brown skin, wet and mud-streaked. Dense
  salt-and-pepper curls swept up and back, grey at temples and front hairline. Days-old
  grey-flecked stubble. **No glasses in this film** — deliberate exception to the standing likeness
  lock, set by Rajan; the face reads through the curls, the long jaw and the level gaze. Wardrobe:
  coarse grey-brown fur wrap over the left shoulder, laced hide kilt to mid-thigh, leather cord
  wraps on both forearms, hide foot-bindings. One **ochre-red woven sash** worn as a scarf around
  neck and shoulders, roughly 1.4 m long, frayed ends — the only saturated red in the film, and the
  sling.
- **THE STONE BEAST (`CRE_STONE_BEAST`)** — 12 m, humanoid, built of fitted basalt slabs with
  molten orange seams glowing between them. Helm-like brow, no face, two furnace-slit eyes. Cinder
  crumbling constantly from shoulders and joints. Steam boils white wherever the stream touches it.
  Moss and torn vine crusted across its back. Formidable and spectacular, never grotesque: it
  cracks and goes dark, it never bleeds.

## World rules
- **Midday, always.** Every shot is `EXT. JUNGLE CLEARING - MIDDAY`. The darkest state in the film
  is cool open shade under the giant; there is no night, no black frame, nothing unreadable.
- **Two figures, ever.** Rajan and the beast. No crowd, no third character, no animal in frame —
  birdsong is off-screen sound only.
- **No bone, no ruin, no architecture anywhere.** The arena is living jungle: a wide shallow stream
  running over black stone through an open clearing, walled and roofed by flowering canopy.
- The black stones are the **beast's own cooled cinders** — they shed glowing, hiss into the water,
  and are black before they settle. Established in shot 2, paid off in shot 5.
- The **fallen forest giant** lies across the far side of the clearing with young flowering trees
  standing out of it, and is visible in every wide shot.
- The scarf never leaves Rajan's hand once the stone is loaded.
