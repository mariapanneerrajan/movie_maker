> ### THE POSITIVE CONSTRUCTION — read this before the three shots
>
> **WHAT THIS IS: A TIDE COMING IN, MADE OF PAPER.**
>
> That is the single sentence gate 4 and gate 5 should carry, and every other instruction below is a
> consequence of it. Give the model something to render, not only things to avoid. The reference
> images are: **an incoming tide across flat sand; wind-driven tumbleweed across a plain; a braided
> river seen from a low bank; grain flowing up a conveyor.** All four are *ordered mass movement in
> one direction, at a constant unhurried pace, made of dry pale material.* None of them is an animal
> and none of them has an intention.
>
> **SIX POSITIVE SPECIFICATIONS, all restated in the node prompt:**
>
> **(1) IT IS PALE AND THE GROUND IS DARK.** Bleached paper, foil and sun-faded film against dark wet
> sand. The current is the BRIGHTEST thing in the frame. Insect grammar is dark-things-on-light-ground;
> this is the exact inverse, and it is the cheapest single fix available.
>
> **(2) EVERYTHING IS BIG.** Each knot is football-sized or larger, and there is a boot or a leg in
> frame in shots 4.11 and 4.13 to prove it. Nothing small, nothing fine, nothing granular. Fear of
> small things moving together is fear of *small*.
>
> **(3) IT MOVES AT A WALKING PACE, CONSTANT, AND NEVER ACCELERATES.** Hopping and trundling, end over
> end, the way litter moves in wind. Nothing darts. Nothing changes direction. Nothing accelerates when
> it gets near him.
>
> **(4) IT ALL GOES ONE WAY, IN ORDERED LANES.** The current has a visible braided grain, like a river
> seen from above. Nothing scatters, nothing mills, nothing converges on anything. A swarm has a
> target; a tide has a direction.
>
> **(5) IT PARTS AROUND HIM AND CLOSES BEHIND HIM.** Water around a rock. NOTHING TOUCHES HIM AT ANY
> POINT IN ANY OF THE THREE SHOTS. Nothing climbs him, nothing brushes him, nothing pauses at him. He
> is not an object of interest to it, and that indifference is what makes the beat sad instead of
> frightening.
>
> **(6) IT IS FEEDING SOMETHING, NOT ATTACKING SOMEONE.** The destination is in frame in shots 4.12 and
> 4.13 — the river, then the legs — so the audience knows where it is going before it has finished
> arriving. A movement whose destination is visible cannot read as a hunt.
>
> **NEGATIVES, secondary and subordinate to the six above:** no legs, no antennae, no segmentation, no
> eyes, no shells, no clicking, no chittering, no insects and no flies anywhere in the film. Never the
> word *swarm*, *scuttle*, *crawl* or *seethe* in any prompt. And never *rot*, *wet* or *slime* — these
> knots are **DRY AND PAPERY**, the same material and the same behaviour as the single knot at 0:04,
> which the audience has already been taught to find harmless because Rajan was bored by it.
>
> **The warmth this arrives fourteen seconds after is protected by the fact that this is not a reversal
> of the tone — it is a reversal of the SCALE. The thing he defeated with a rinsed bottle at 0:22 has
> arrived in its millions. That is grief, not threat, and it is why he does not move.**
