# craft-thumbnail

Read by: whoever drafts the thumbnail prompt (stage 7). Read `CRAFT.md` first.

## Founding principles

### What the thumbnail competes against

A YouTube or LinkedIn thumbnail is seen at roughly 350 pixels wide, in a grid, for under a
second, next to a dozen others. It is not a frame from the film. It is a poster, and it obeys
poster rules.

### The three things it must do, in order

1. **Read instantly at small size.** One clear focal subject, high contrast against its
   background, uncluttered silhouette. Squint at it — if the subject dissolves, it fails.
2. **Promise the film honestly.** The essence of the story in one image. A thumbnail that
   over-promises spectacle the film does not contain is the fastest way to lose an audience's
   trust, and it violates the workshop's whole purpose.
3. **Be beautiful.** Epic, imaginative, worth stopping for.

### Composition that survives the grid

- Subject occupying the left or right third, title in the opposing space — never centred and
  fighting.
- Strong value separation: the subject's silhouette must be distinguishable from the background
  with colour removed.
- Depth in three clear planes — subject, mid-ground story element, sky or vista behind.
- Avoid small detail anywhere. Anything under about 3% of frame width vanishes.

### Rajan must be prominent and recognisable

`CHR_RAJAN` is the face of every film in this workshop. In the thumbnail he is large, lit
flatteringly, and recognisably himself — the same likeness locks as his character sheet. Face
turned enough to read as a person, not a silhouette. This is the one place his face should be
unmistakable at a glance.

### The title as designed type

Name the type treatment explicitly: weight, case, era, material, and how it sits in the frame.
"Heavy condensed sans in weathered brass, all caps, lower right, integrated into the haze" is
directable. "Nice title text" is not.

Keep the title to the words of the chosen title alone — no taglines, no subtitles, no channel
name. Every additional word costs legibility.

Note the caveat from `craft-seedance.md`: image models render text imperfectly. Expect to fix the
title in a spot-correction pass (Gemini Omni Flash) or to composite it. The prompt should still
specify the treatment, so the generated image leaves the right space for it.

### Lighting and colour

Unlike the asset renders, this is fully graded and dramatic. Push contrast and saturation beyond
the film's own grade — a thumbnail that matches the film's subtlety disappears in a feed. Rim
light on the subject to separate them from the background is the single highest-value move.

### Standards still apply

Family audience, always. Eye-catching never means fear-baiting, and the epic scale on offer here
is plenty without it.

### Format

16:9, 1920×1080 minimum. Specify it in the prompt.
