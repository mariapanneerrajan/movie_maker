                            TEN THOUSAND STAVES


                                 Written by
                             the Movie Workshop


                          30 seconds · three acts
                                2026-08-14


FADE IN:


                                ACT ONE


1.                                             0:00-0:06

EXT. VAELUR SHORE - DAWN

Black water under a cold band of amber.

The sea LIFTS. An immense ridged back breaks the
surface and keeps rising -- a hill of dark hide,
water pouring off it in curtains, pale light moving
beneath the skin like coals under ash.

It slides under without a sound.

The swell it leaves comes in, and comes in, and
rolls RAJAN up the sand on his face. Salt-crusted.
Nothing in his hands.

His fingers close on a long fighting staff in the
wash. He drives it into the wet sand and hauls
himself up onto his knees.

The staff stands. Behind it, the sea goes flat.


                                ACT TWO


2.                                             0:06-0:12

EXT. RIDGE ABOVE VAELUR - SUNRISE

Rajan reaches the crest and stops. He looks back
the way he came.

The sea is glass now. Empty to the edge of the
world. No sail, no wake, nothing that could carry a
man anywhere.

He turns inland.

VAELUR stacks up above him -- black basalt terraces
climbing out of the surf, and crowning them the
open bowl of an arena cut into the rock, banners
snapping over its rim.

Along the wall-walk, sentries turn to watch him
come. One staff goes up. Then a dozen. Then the
whole line of them.

He is a small figure on a long white road, walking
in.


3.                                             0:12-0:18

EXT. VAELUR ARENA - MORNING

A packed bowl of faces held in shadow. The sun has
not cleared the west wall. The floor is grey dust.

Rajan walks out onto it, staff carried low.

Across from him THE CHAMPION rises -- a head
taller, chalked white to the shoulders, his stave
already turning up to speed.

They meet. The staves CRACK -- once, three times,
then a blur, dust jumping off the stone in rings.

Rajan turns inside a strike, sweeps the big man's
legs, and puts him flat on his back.

Rajan's staff comes up over his head.

The bowl comes to its feet.


4.                                             0:18-0:24

EXT. VAELUR ARENA - CONTINUOUS

Ten thousand staves STAMP the stone in time.
Faster. They want the finish.

Rajan holds there, arms locked over his head.
Something goes out of his face.

He lowers the staff and steps back off the fallen
man.

The stamping stops. The silence is worse.

Then, on the lowest tier, one stave comes up. The
tier above answers it. The rise runs around the
bowl and climbs it, ring after ring, until every
stave in Vaelur is up and levelled at the floor.

Rajan stands alone at the bottom of it, no bigger
than a nail.


                               ACT THREE


5.                                             0:24-0:28

EXT. VAELUR ARENA - CONTINUOUS

CLOSE ON Rajan. We PUSH IN.

He plants his staff upright in the dust beside him
and lets go of it.

Then he opens both hands, palms out, and holds them
open. He is not braced for anything.

The sun clears the west wall. Light crosses his
face from off frame, warm and level.

Behind him, soft, the champion comes onto his knees
and lays his stave down flat.

And the forest behind that begins to come down -- a
wave of vertical lines going horizontal, tier after
tier, climbing away out of focus.


6.                                             0:28-0:30

EXT. VAELUR ARENA - CONTINUOUS

The staff, upright in the dust. We RISE.

The floor, the tiers, the terraces, the roofs
beyond the wall and the flat gold sea past them --
all of it laid over with staves set down.

One vertical thing left standing in the city.

                                              FADE OUT.
