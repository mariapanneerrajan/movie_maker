```
FADE IN:


                                ACT ONE


1.                                                          0:00-0:06

EXT. THE WHITE CITY, CENTRAL PLAZA - DUSK

A hand fills the sky. Chalk-white porcelain plates over a
churning core of blue smoke. Each finger ends in a claw
longer than the tower beneath it. Unhurried, it closes on
nothing.

We CRANE DOWN the wrist, the arm, the lantern ribs of a body
two hundred metres tall. A long-jawed head hung low over the
streets. Two eyes like ice-blue cracks under a heavy brow.

Threads of ice-blue light rise out of the streets in their
thousands, thin as spider silk, and sink into its back. Where
they land the porcelain swells. It grows a storey taller.

The crane keeps falling. Past glass towers with whole forests
standing on their terraces. Past footbridges hung with vines.
Down to a wide green plaza and its running water.

Hundreds of silhouettes are pressed low there under the trees,
heads down, threads pouring off their shoulders in a slow blue
weather.

One figure stands up out of them. RAJAN, late thirties, lean,
faded oatmeal work coveralls and a worn leather tool harness.

A thread comes off his chest too. Thin, and shaking. He is as
afraid as every person behind him. He is looking up anyway.


                                ACT TWO


2.                                                          0:06-0:15

EXT. THE WHITE CITY, CENTRAL PLAZA - DUSK

Behind Rajan a blast shutter DROPS across the glass pavilion
-- one iron note that rolls out across the water. The shelter
seals. Everyone else is on the other side of it.

He turns his back on it and walks. Eyes down on the wet
paving. Hands shaking at his sides until he shuts them into
fists.

Other people's threads pour up around him in a rising blue
curtain. His own whips off him and joins them, brighter with
every step, feeding the thing he is walking toward.

Halfway across the plaza his knee goes. He stops. He cannot
make the next step, and he does not pretend otherwise -- head
down, chest heaving, one long look back at the sealed
shutter.

He flattens a hand over his own chest, over the thread, and
holds it there. He breathes out.

Then he steps forward again.

Far above, the long head swings round on its neck. Its shadow
comes across the plaza toward him like a door swinging shut.


3.                                                          0:15-0:24

EXT. THE WHITE CITY, CENTRAL PLAZA - DUSK

The shadow covers him. Rajan stops inside it -- and lifts his
eyes off the ground into the face of the thing.

The thread on his chest turns gold from the sternum up. It
breaks, and falls past him like an ember, and the giant gets
nothing.

The colossus recoils. It loses a storey. It loses another.
The glaze goes dull in wide grey patches down its ribs.

Then it hauls. Every blue thread in the city snaps taut at
once -- whole avenues of light tearing up off the rooftops
into it. Terrace by terrace, the green goes out of the city
and the leaves turn grey.

It swells past the towers, past the cloud. Its jaw comes open
on a throat full of blue fire, and it ROARS at the sky.

The shockwave takes Rajan off his feet. He goes down hard.
His glasses skitter away across the wet paving.

He gets to his knees, gropes for them, blind. His face comes
apart -- mouth open, shoulders up around his ears.

The gold is gone. A thick blue thread pours off his chest,
straight up into the dark, and the giant drinks it.


                               ACT THREE


4.                                                          0:24-0:28

EXT. THE WHITE CITY, CENTRAL PLAZA - DUSK

Warm gold light comes low across the water and the wet paving
from behind him. The shutter is up.

They come out. Silhouettes streaming past Rajan on both
sides, and stopping there, between him and the giant, faces
up. Their threads turn gold in a wave that runs the length of
the plaza and away up every terrace.

The blue goes out of the city like a tide going out, and the
green comes back up behind it.

A hand comes into the frame, open, and waits. Rajan takes
hold of it and gets up.

With nothing left to drink, the colossus unravels from the
head down, plates peeling away into nothing, and comes down
out of the sky.


5.                                                          0:28-0:30

EXT. THE WHITE CITY, CENTRAL PLAZA - DUSK

A hand fills the frame. His. Cupped, lit from below by the
small work lamp on his harness.

In it: the creature. Palm-sized. Chalk-white, dull, no smoke
left in it. The same long jaw, shut. Teeth like grass seed.
Two dim sparks where the fire was. It is trembling.

So is his hand.

It closes, slowly, around it.


                                                          FADE OUT.
```
