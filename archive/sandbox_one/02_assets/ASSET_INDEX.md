# ASSET INDEX — THE SHRINKING GIANT

All four character sheets share one panel layout, mirroring `protagonist/RAJAN_RAW.png`: tall
vertical panels on flat mid-grey — **front / back / close-up** — with no profile view, no
three-quarter view, and no height scale. `CHR_COLOSSUS` carries one extra panel for the open jaw,
explained in its own file.

**Saturation rule, from `01_look.md`, carried into every relevant prompt:** the fear-thread is the
film's only **emissive** colour; the greenery is its only **reflective** colour. Foliage sits low
in the luminance range and is never lit from within, and neither the crowd's wardrobe nor Rajan's
enters either register — nothing worn by anyone is blue, gold or green.

**Downstream notes for the script prompt engineer:**
- No profile or three-quarter reference of Rajan's face exists on the raw sheet or in this asset
  set. A side-on angle of him in the node is an extrapolation, not a sourced view.
- The colossus's jaw is shut in every frame of the film except the roar at 0:21, and **no shot may
  frame the open jaw and a human being in the same composition.**
- `ENV_WHITE_CITY_PLAZA` and `ENV_WHITE_CITY_DRAINED` are the same place in two **material**
  states, not two places and not two times of day. Same geometry, same framing, same fixed props —
  connect the one the moment calls for, never both in the same shot.
- **Rajan's chest stays clear from sternum to collar in every frame.** The fear-thread lifts from
  that field, and nothing — strap, buckle, lamp, hand or prop — may cross or hide it.
