**Source-sheet note:** Panelled to match the protagonist sheet — three vertical panels on flat
grey, front / back / close-up, no profile view, no height scale. Two conventions do not transfer.
The headless front crop is dropped: on Rajan's sheet it isolates one canonical face, and these
faces are deliberately generic. And panel three is the offered open hand rather than a headshot,
because the look sheet forbids a featured face in the crowd — a crowd headshot would create the
individual the film must not have, while the open hand entering frame at 0:26 is a shot the script
does need.

**Wardrobe note:** rebuilt for the revised city. The palette is deliberately low-chroma warm
neutrals — undyed and plant-dyed cloth — so the crowd makes no third colour statement: it stays
out of the emissive register the fear-threads own and out of the reflective green register the
foliage owns. Interest is carried by weave, drape and layering rather than by colour, which also
keeps the silhouettes clean, since that is how the crowd is framed in every shot.
