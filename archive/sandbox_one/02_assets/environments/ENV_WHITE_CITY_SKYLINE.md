## Prompt

```
A high aerial architectural reference of an invented glass-and-garden city, seen from above the
towers, with no people in it. The city is thriving, cultivated and clearly loved.

Slender curved towers rise in loose clusters, built in warm low-iron glass with floor-to-sky
curtain walls and hairline steel framing, tapering and rounding as they climb. Their terraces step
back at every level and carry deep planted beds — mature trees standing in soil on the terraces
and on the flat rooftops, whole small forests high above the ground, with vines spilling down
entire facades. Glass footbridges span between the towers at many heights, hung underneath with
trailing gardens. Between the towers, straight avenues run through the city in a loose radial
pattern, paved seamlessly in pale stone and glass, wet and faintly reflective, with lines of
planting and shallow water channels down their length. At the centre of the radial pattern sits a
wide unroofed open plaza with trees standing in it, read here at distance as the hub the avenues
run into.

There are no stone columns, no colonnades, no arcades and no cement anywhere in the city. All
foliage sits low in the luminance range — deep viridian, moss and sage, desaturated and
reflective, never vivid emerald and never lit from within. Every surface is blank, with no
signage, lettering, writing, logos, flags or numerals anywhere.

Render the whole city under flat, even, neutral white overcast illumination, uniform from every
direction, with no directional key, no rim and no cast shadows beyond soft ambient contact
shading. Keep geometry, materials and scale clearly readable, sharp and evenly focused from the
foreground rooftops to the far towers.

Negative: no colour grade, film emulation, time of day, sunset, dusk, night, weather, clouds,
fog, haze, atmosphere, god rays, lens flare, bloom, vignette, depth-of-field blur, aerial
perspective fade, motion blur, directional dramatic lighting or mood. No columns, arcades or
stone construction. No people, creatures, aircraft, vehicles, damage or destruction. No glowing
threads, no emissive foliage, no text or signage.
```
